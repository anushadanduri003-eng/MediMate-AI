# 🏥 AI Health Copilot – Intelligent Healthcare Assistance

## 📌 Project Overview

AI Health Copilot is an AI-powered healthcare assistance platform designed to make basic health guidance and exercise monitoring more accessible. It aims to help students, elderly people, general users, and individuals recovering from injuries or surgery access personalized health information and movement guidance.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many people lack easy access to personalized health guidance, proper exercise instructions, and timely support for identifying when professional medical attention may be needed. Healthcare professionals can also spend significant time reviewing routine exercise activities.

Our solution aims to support users with AI-assisted health information, exercise guidance, and movement analysis while helping healthcare professionals review cases that may require further attention.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

AI Health Copilot provides a user-friendly platform that combines AI assistance and healthcare-focused features to support health awareness, exercise guidance, and movement monitoring.
